An AI-first university with a complete campus.

Alpa University is built around Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. Students learn Python, data science and model building, then train real robots in our lab. Alongside our tech programs we offer BBA, HRM, English, Pak Studies, Physics, Chemistry and Botany, so every student gets a well-rounded education.
